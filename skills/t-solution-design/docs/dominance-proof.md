# Dominance Proof Policy

A single option is not automatically justified by lack of imagination. The package must show alternatives that were actively considered and eliminated.

Each eliminated alternative must state:

1. its mechanism;
2. the exact constraint or invariant it violates;
3. supporting evidence;
4. why mitigation is insufficient;
5. what changed condition would make it viable.

A dominance conclusion is falsifiable and remains subject to human critique.
