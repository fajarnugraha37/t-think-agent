# Reasoning and Evidence Policy

The skill exposes auditable structured rationale, not private token-by-token chain-of-thought.

For a material inference record:

1. supporting facts and investigation inference IDs;
2. the transformation applied to those facts;
3. alternative explanations;
4. contradicting evidence or explicit absence thereof;
5. falsification conditions;
6. confidence and limitations.

Conversation context may suggest a search but is not system evidence. Generated model artifacts do not become independent proof of the claims that produced them.
