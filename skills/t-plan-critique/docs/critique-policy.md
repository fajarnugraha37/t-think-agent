# Critique policy

1. Critique is evidence to evaluate, not a command.
2. Every critique must be assessed exactly once.
3. `REJECTED_WITH_EVIDENCE` requires reviewed evidence and contradicting findings.
4. Accepted plan changes become unapplied `PREV-*` directives.
5. Evidence gaps become `PEREQ-*` requests.
6. No upstream artifact is edited during critique.
7. Human approval is required to freeze a plan.
